01 · Overview

Project Overview

High-level snapshot of what Anon E-Commerce is and who it serves.

Identity
Anon E-Commerce

A multi-vendor marketplace where independent sellers list and manage their own products, buyers shop across every seller in one storefront, and admins govern the whole platform.

4 Roles
User Types
  • 2. Buyer — shops, orders, reviews
  • 3. Seller — lists and fulfills products
  • 4. Admin — governs the platform
